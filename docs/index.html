How it works

You hold the port half, the rival holds starboard. Scrap builds defenses on your deck plating; every turret draws reactor power (1, 2, 3 per tier) from a budget of 24. Biomass regenerates and pays for drop pods aimed at the rival's hull breaches; regen, cap, and swarm HP all climb as the match runs, and every pod you drop adds a little regen for good. Bulkheads seal corridors and vents for 90 seconds and block sight, but every breach must keep a route to the core. Ducts (marked D1, D2) are linked pairs: the swarm crawls in one mouth, hidden and untargetable, and climbs out of the other, usually near a reactor. Seal either mouth to close the duct. Sealed hatches open at 2:00. EMP and cutter pods land anywhere on the rival deck. First reactor to zero loses; after five minutes both reactors bleed.
